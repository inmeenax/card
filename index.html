QUICK GUIDE

How to play Call Break

Goal

Call how many tricks you expect to win. You play 5 rounds, with 13 cards and 13 tricks in each round.

Card strength

A is high, 2 is low. Spades are always trump and beat other suits. If no spade is played, the highest card in the led suit wins.

Playing cards

Follow the led suit if you can. In this version, if you can beat the current winning card in that suit, you must overtake. If you cannot follow suit, play a spade if you have one; overtrump an existing spade when possible.

Scoring

Meet your call: score your bid. Win extra tricks: add 0.1 per extra trick. Miss your call: lose points equal to your bid. Highest total after five rounds wins.

These are configurable house rules. Call Break rules can differ by region and platform.
